01Who we are

Flipshift is a game made by Jason Worden, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is involved when you play Flipshift and how it is used. If you have any questions, contact us at the address at the bottom of this page.

02Information saved on your device

Flipshift saves your game progress on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off. This includes your best score, coins, level and XP, unlocked and equipped skins, daily mission progress, and settings such as sound on or off.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

03Game Center leaderboard

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, Flipshift sends your scores to Apple so they can appear on the global leaderboard. Your Game Center nickname and avatar are shown on the leaderboard according to your Game Center settings.

Game Center is run by Apple and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple ID, email, or other account details. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

04Advertising

Flipshift is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads you choose to watch, for example to continue a run or double your coins. Others are shown occasionally between runs.

To show and measure ads, Google's advertising software may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as an identifier for this app on your device
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (country or city, not your exact location)
  • Device information, such as model, operating system, and language
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Flipshift does not ask for Apple's permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so your device's advertising identifier is not used for cross-app tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

05What we don't do

  • We don't ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or exact location.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell or rent anyone's information.

06Children

Flipshift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through Flipshift, contact us and we will help.

07Your choices

  • You can choose not to watch rewarded ads. They are always optional.
  • You can manage ad personalization and tracking in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security.
  • You can sign out of Game Center in Settings to stop sending scores to the leaderboard.
  • You can delete all game data by deleting the app.

08Changes to this policy

If we change how Flipshift handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
