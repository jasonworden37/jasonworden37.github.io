02Common questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to flip gravity between the floor and the ceiling. Dodge the walls, grab coins and power-ups, and see how far you can go. The game speeds up as your score climbs.

What do the power-ups do?

Tap the lightning bolt on the title screen for a guide to every power-up.

Where is my progress saved?

Your best score, coins, skins, level and missions are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes this progress, and it does not transfer to a new phone.

When do daily missions reset?

You get three new missions every day at midnight, your local time.

Why doesn't my score show on the leaderboard?

The global leaderboard uses Apple Game Center. Make sure you're signed in under Settings → Game Center, then finish a run.

Why don't I see an ad option sometimes?

Optional ads (continue a run, double coins, ad skins) only appear when an ad is available. Try again in a minute or check your internet connection.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button in the top corner. The game also stays quiet when your iPhone's silent switch is on.
